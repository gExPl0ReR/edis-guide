EDIS GmbH is committed to operating its business in a responsible, transparent, and ethical manner. The EDIS Code of Ethics and Business Conduct sets out the principles that guide how the company deals with customers, employees, partners, and suppliers. Compliance with the code is mandatory for EDIS GmbH and all of its business partners.

## Core Principles

### 1. Sustainable Business Practices

EDIS is committed to sustainable and responsible business practices. The company contributes to the protection of the environment and to a sustainable digital infrastructure through its services and products. All actions are grounded in the principles of integrity and accountability — towards customers, employees, and partners alike.

### 2. Legal and Regulatory Compliance

EDIS GmbH strictly adheres to all applicable national and international laws, with particular attention to **data protection law (GDPR)**, IT security regulations, and corporate governance requirements. All business activities comply with competition and antitrust law, and EDIS does not seek unfair competitive advantages.

### 3. Conflicts of Interest

Employees and partners of EDIS are required to avoid any conflicts of interest. Business decisions are always made in the best interests of the company and its customers, without personal considerations influencing the outcome.

### 4. Labour Standards and Human Rights

EDIS respects internationally recognised human rights and ensures fair and safe working conditions for all employees. The company firmly rejects all forms of discrimination, forced labour, and child labour, and is committed to maintaining a respectful working environment.

### 5. Environmental Responsibility

As an IT service provider, EDIS works to minimise resource consumption and reduce environmental impact. The company uses energy-efficient server technology and sustainable hosting solutions. Environmental protection is an integral part of how EDIS runs its operations.

### 6. Health and Safety

EDIS places a high priority on the safety and wellbeing of its employees. The company provides safe working conditions and promotes safety awareness through regular training. Its technical infrastructure is designed to meet the highest safety standards.

### 7. Supply Chain Due Diligence

EDIS works only with partners and suppliers who are equally committed to upholding human rights, environmental protection, and fair business practices. The company maintains a transparent and responsible supply chain.

### 8. Data Protection and Privacy

The security and protection of data is a top priority at EDIS GmbH. All data is processed in strict compliance with the GDPR. EDIS uses modern encryption technologies to ensure the integrity and confidentiality of customer data.

### 9. Technical Compliance

EDIS ensures that all products and services meet applicable technical compliance requirements. This covers product security, adherence to security and data protection standards, and the protection of intellectual property.

### 10. Business Continuity

EDIS maintains comprehensive risk management processes and backup solutions to ensure uninterrupted service. Regular testing of emergency and recovery plans enables the company to maintain smooth operations even in the event of unexpected incidents.

## Reporting Violations

Employees, partners, and stakeholders are encouraged to report any suspected violation of this code of conduct. EDIS provides appropriate channels for such reports and supports all investigations into potential breaches. Violations may result in measures up to and including termination of the business relationship.

For questions about the EDIS Code of Ethics, or to raise a concern, contact [hosting@edis.at](mailto:hosting@edis.at).
