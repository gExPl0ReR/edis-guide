If you have a domain registered at EDIS and want to use it with a Jimdo website, you can connect the two by updating the DNS records in the EDIS DNS portal — no nameserver transfer is required. This approach also lets you keep your email running through EDIS while your website is served by Jimdo.

Before you begin, confirm which Jimdo product you are using: **Jimdo Creator** and the newer **Jimdo Website Builder** have different admin interfaces, and the exact DNS values may differ. Check the connection settings in your Jimdo account to get the current, authoritative DNS values for your plan.

## Preparing Jimdo for Your Domain

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add Your Domain in Jimdo">
    Log in to your Jimdo account and navigate to the domain settings for the website you want to connect. In Jimdo Creator, go to **Domains and Emails → Domains → Add New Domain → Use Existing Domain** and enter your EDIS domain name. This step requires a paid Jimdo plan that supports external domain connections.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Copy the DNS Values from Jimdo">
    After adding the domain, Jimdo will display the DNS values you need to configure. For Jimdo Creator, the typical requirements are:

    - A **CNAME record** for `www` pointing to `web.jimdo.com.`
    - A **redirect** (or forwarding rule) from the apex/root domain (without `www`) to the `www` address

    Copy these values exactly as shown in your Jimdo account — they may have changed since this guide was written.

    <Note>
      A CNAME record target is always a **hostname** (e.g. `web.jimdo.com.`), never an IP address. Do not enter a hostname into an A record field — A records require an IPv4 address. If Jimdo gives you an IP address, use an A record; if it gives you a hostname, use a CNAME.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Updating DNS Records at EDIS

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log In to the EDIS DNS Portal">
    Open the [EDIS DNS portal](https://pdns.edis.global/login) and sign in. Select the DNS zone for your domain.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add or Update the www CNAME Record">
    Add a **CNAME record** for `www` with the target hostname provided by Jimdo (typically `web.jimdo.com.`). If a CNAME or A record for `www` already exists, update it to the new value.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure the Root Domain Redirect">
    Jimdo requires visitors who reach your root domain (e.g. `yourdomain.at`) to be redirected to `www.yourdomain.at`. Set this up using the domain redirect feature in the EDIS vHost management panel at [manage.edis.at/vhost/](https://manage.edis.at/vhost/).

    <Warning>
      An HTTPS redirect from the root domain requires a valid SSL certificate for the root domain. Make sure an SSL certificate is in place before enabling an HTTPS redirect, otherwise visitors will see a certificate error.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Preserve Your Email DNS Records">
    Do **not** delete or replace your MX, SPF, DKIM, or DMARC records when making these changes. These records keep your EDIS email working. A nameserver change is not required and should be avoided, as it would move DNS control away from EDIS and could affect your email routing.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Wait for Propagation and Verify">
    Jimdo states that a CNAME-based domain connection may take up to **24 hours** to fully propagate. Check both your root domain and the `www` subdomain, verify that HTTPS works, and send a test email to confirm that mail delivery is unaffected.

    If anything looks wrong, check the actual DNS values published in the EDIS portal first before making additional changes — repeated untested edits make diagnosing problems harder.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  For the latest step-by-step instructions from Jimdo, refer to the official Jimdo guide: [Connect an external domain to your Jimdo website via CNAME](https://help.jimdo.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005537866-Connect-an-external-domain-to-your-Jimdo-website-via-CNAME).
</Tip>

## Keeping Email at EDIS

Your email continues to flow through EDIS as long as your MX record still points to `mx.edis.at` and your SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records remain intact. Connecting to Jimdo via CNAME only affects the `www` subdomain and does not touch the mail-related DNS entries. If you have any doubt about which records to keep, contact EDIS support at [hosting@edis.at](mailto:hosting@edis.at) before making changes.
