---
title: 'How to Reset Your Password for the EDIS Customer Portal'
sidebarTitle: 'Reset Password'
description: 'Learn how to reset your login password for the EDIS customer portal at manage.edis.at using your customer number and email address.'
---

If you have forgotten your password for the EDIS customer portal, you can reset it at any time using your customer number. The reset link is sent to the email address registered in your EDIS account, so make sure you have access to that inbox before you begin.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Password Reset Page">
    Navigate to the [EDIS password reset page](https://manage.edis.at/passwd/). You do not need to be logged in to access this page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter Your Customer Number">
    Type your **customer number** into the input field and submit the form. You can find your customer number in the top-right corner of any EDIS service invoice.

    Once you submit, the system sends a password reset email to the address stored in your EDIS account.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click the Link in the Email">
    Open the reset email and click the link it contains. The link takes you to a secure page where you can choose a new password. Follow the on-screen instructions to set your new password.

    <Note>
      The reset link expires after a short time. If you do not use it promptly, repeat the process to request a fresh link.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Log In with Your New Password">
    Go to the [EDIS customer portal](https://manage.edis.at/client/) and sign in using your **customer number** and the **new password** you just set.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you are still unable to log in after resetting your password, contact EDIS support at [hosting@edis.at](mailto:hosting@edis.at). Include your customer number so the team can locate your account quickly.
</Tip>
