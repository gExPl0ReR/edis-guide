EDIS offers two ways to cancel your domains and web hosting products: directly through the customer portal, or in writing by submitting a cancellation form to the support team. Regardless of which method you choose, cancellations are subject to a notice period of **six weeks before the end of the current service period**, so plan accordingly to avoid an unwanted renewal charge.

<Warning>
  Cancelling a web hosting package permanently deletes all website files, databases, and email addresses associated with that package at the end of the service period. Back up all data you want to keep **before** the cancellation date. A domain cancellation is handled separately from a hosting cancellation — cancelling one does not automatically cancel the other.
</Warning>

## Method 1: Cancel via the Customer Portal

The quickest way to cancel is through your EDIS customer portal, where you can see your exact contract end date before confirming.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log In to the Customer Portal">
    Sign in at [manage.edis.at/client/](https://manage.edis.at/client/). You will see a dashboard listing all your active products along with their **"Billed until"** dates.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Locate the Product to Cancel">
    Find the domain or hosting package you want to cancel. Review the billing period shown so you know exactly when the service ends.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Click the Cancel Button">
    Click the **"Cancel"** button next to the product. The system calculates and displays the exact cancellation date based on the applicable notice period. Confirm the cancellation to register it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Save the Confirmation">
    Keep the confirmation email or reference number you receive. You can use it later to verify the cancellation status.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Method 2: Cancel in Writing

If you prefer a written cancellation, or if the cancel button is not yet available in the portal (for example, on a newly provisioned product), you can submit a cancellation form by email.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the Correct Form">
    Download the appropriate cancellation form from the [EDIS forms page](https://www.edis.at/de/support-und-faq/formulare):

    - **Domains:** use the domain cancellation form
    - **Hosting packages:** use the hosting cancellation form
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete and Sign the Form">
    Fill in the form carefully. Domain cancellations must be signed by the **domain holder**; hosting cancellations must be signed by the **contract holder**. Specify the exact service and the desired end date.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Submit the Form to EDIS">
    Send the completed, signed form to [hosting@edis.at](mailto:hosting@edis.at) or [fax@edis.at](mailto:fax@edis.at). Include the domain name or hosting product reference so the support team can match your request.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm the End Date">
    Wait for a written confirmation from EDIS that names the specific service and the confirmed end date. Do not assume the cancellation is processed until you receive this confirmation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Before You Cancel: Checklist

Before cancelling any hosting package, take the time to audit everything that relies on it.

- **Identify all affected services** — a single hosting package may host multiple domains, websites, and email addresses. List each one and decide whether it will move to a new provider or be discontinued.
- **Back up files and databases** — download all website files and export all databases before the cancellation date.
- **Migrate email** — a domain transfer does not carry email messages with it. Export any emails you need and set up the new mail service in advance.
- **Update DNS records** — after migrating a site or mailbox, update the A, MX, and other DNS records to point to the new provider before the old service goes offline.
- **Verify the cancellation confirmation** — confirm that the end date in the EDIS confirmation matches the date you requested.

<Note>
  Cancelling a hosting package does not automatically cancel any domain registrations attached to it, and vice versa. Always confirm which specific services are covered by each cancellation request.
</Note>
