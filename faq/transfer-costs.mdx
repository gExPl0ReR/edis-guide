When you transfer a domain to EDIS, a line item labelled **"Transfer costs"** (Transferkosten) may appear on your EDIS service invoice. This charge is a normal part of the transfer process for most domain extensions and is not a duplicate billing error. Understanding what it covers helps you verify that your invoice is correct.

## What Transfer Costs Cover

For generic top-level domains such as `.com`, `.net`, and `.org`, a successful registrar transfer typically extends the domain registration by **one year** from the current expiry date. The transfer fee you pay covers both the transfer process itself and this additional year of registration.

**Example:** If your domain currently expires on 31 October 2026 and you complete a transfer with a one-year extension, the new expiry date becomes 31 October 2027. The charge on your invoice reflects that renewed registration period.

<Note>
  The exact rules vary by domain extension and registry. Special conditions may apply if your domain was renewed automatically very recently, or if you are approaching the maximum allowed registration period. Always compare the invoice amount against the order price shown at the time of transfer.
</Note>

## Verifying the Charge

To confirm that a transfer fee is correct, check the following three values on your invoice and in the customer portal:

1. **Order price** — the fee quoted when you initiated the transfer
2. **Previous expiry date** — the date before the transfer was completed
3. **New expiry date** — the date confirmed after the transfer

If all three values are consistent and the new expiry date is one year beyond the previous one, the charge is correct.

## Included Domains and Hosting Packages

Some EDIS hosting packages include a free domain. Whether the transfer of an existing domain counts as the included domain depends on the specific offer you booked and how the domain is assigned to your package. Check the terms of your hosting plan to clarify this.

<Tip>
  If a charge on your invoice is unclear, email [hosting@edis.at](mailto:hosting@edis.at) with the **domain name**, the **order reference**, and the **line item in question**. The EDIS support team can review the billing details and explain exactly what was charged and why.
</Tip>
