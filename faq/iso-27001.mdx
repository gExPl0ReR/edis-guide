EDIS GmbH operates its hosting and email infrastructure in line with internationally recognised information security standards. This page summarises the security measures and certifications relevant to customers using EDIS web hosting and email services.

<Note>
  This page is an informational overview. For binding commitments, refer to your individual service contract and the data processing agreement (Auftragsverarbeitungsvereinbarung). You can download the current data processing agreement at [manage.edis.at](https://manage.edis.at/client/ADV_EU-DSGVO-EDIS_GmbH.pdf).
</Note>

## ISO 27001 and ISO 22301

ISO 27001 is the leading international standard for **Information Security Management Systems (ISMS)**. Certification means that an independent auditor has verified that the certified organisation follows a systematic, risk-based approach to protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information.

If you need formal ISO 27001 or ISO 22301 certificates for a supplier audit or compliance review, contact EDIS support at [hosting@edis.at](mailto:hosting@edis.at) and specify:

- The certificate holder and scope you need
- The relevant service or location
- The validity period required for your review

<Note>
  Certifications held by a data centre operator are distinct from a certification held by EDIS GmbH itself. Confirm which entity's certificate you need before requesting documentation.
</Note>

## Encrypted Data Transfer

EDIS supports industry-standard encrypted transfer protocols for all hosting services:

| Protocol | Use Case |
|---|---|
| **HTTPS / TLS** | Websites and the customer portal |
| **FTPS** | FTP with TLS encryption (not the same as SFTP) |
| **SFTP** | Secure file transfer over SSH |
| **SSH** | Command-line access to hosting accounts |

Check your specific hosting plan for the exact access methods included. SFTP and SSH availability depends on the plan tier.

## Backups and Data Retention

EDIS describes daily backups for current web hosting products, with up to **40 daily snapshots** retained. The availability of a specific backup snapshot, the scope of data included, the restore process, and any associated costs must be confirmed for your individual contract.

Do not assume that a backup snapshot will be available indefinitely after a service cancellation. Back up your own data before the end of your service period.

## Infrastructure Redundancy

EDIS hosting infrastructure includes redundant power, cooling, network connectivity, and hardware components. The specific redundancy measures in place for your service are described in the current technical service description for your plan. Older infrastructure specifications should not be used as a current guarantee without confirmation.

[https://manage.edis.at/client/ADV_EU-DSGVO-EDIS_GmbH.pdf](https://manage.edis.at/client/ADV_EU-DSGVO-EDIS_GmbH.pdf)
