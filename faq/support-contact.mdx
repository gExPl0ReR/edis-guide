</Note>

## Tips for Faster Support

Providing the right information upfront reduces back-and-forth and speeds up resolution:

- **Customer number** — found in the top-right corner of any EDIS service invoice
- **Domain name(s)** affected
- **A clear description of the issue** — include error messages verbatim, the steps you took before the issue occurred, and any recent changes you made (DNS updates, password changes, etc.)
- **Screenshots** — for visual issues or error messages, a screenshot sent via WhatsApp or Messenger can save time

If you are contacting support about a billing matter, include the invoice reference number and the specific line item in question.
