The EDIS vHost area is the control panel where you manage your web hosting configuration — including domains, subdomains, PHP settings, databases, email accounts, and more. You can reach it in two ways: by navigating there from the main customer portal, or by logging in directly if you have already set a dedicated vHost password.

## Log In via the Customer Portal

The most common way to access the vHost area is through your EDIS customer account at [manage.edis.at/client/](https://manage.edis.at/client/). This method works immediately — no separate vHost password is required.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Customer Portal">
    Go to [manage.edis.at/client/](https://manage.edis.at/client/) and sign in with your **customer number** (a five-digit number) and your **customer password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to vHost">
    In the left-hand navigation bar, click **vHost**. A list of your web hosting packages appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the vHost Panel">
    Click the **green arrow** next to the hosting package you want to manage. A confirmation pop-up appears — click **OK** (or **Confirm**) to proceed.

    You are now logged in to the vHost management area for that package.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you manage multiple hosting packages, each one has its own green arrow. Make sure you click the arrow next to the correct package before confirming.
</Note>

## Log In Directly to the vHost Area

You can also reach the vHost panel directly at [manage.edis.at/vhost/](https://manage.edis.at/vhost/) without going through the customer portal first. This requires a **separate vHost password** that is distinct from your customer portal password.

### Set Your vHost Password First

Before you can use the direct login, you must set a vHost password inside the customer portal:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to the Customer Portal">
    Go to [manage.edis.at/client/](https://manage.edis.at/client/) and log in with your customer number and customer password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open vHost Settings">
    In the left-hand navigation bar, click **vHost** to see your hosting packages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit the Hosting Package">
    Click the **pencil (edit) icon** next to the hosting package for which you want to set a vHost password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the vHost Password">
    Enter and confirm a new password for direct vHost access, then save your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Sign In Directly

Once your vHost password is set, you can log in at any time without the intermediate customer portal step:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Direct Login Page">
    Navigate to [manage.edis.at/vhost/](https://manage.edis.at/vhost/).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Credentials">
    Provide your **vHost username** (typically the same as your hosting username) and the **vHost password** you set in the previous section, then click **Login**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  The direct login is convenient if you work in the vHost area frequently. Bookmark [manage.edis.at/vhost/](https://manage.edis.at/vhost/) for quick access.
</Tip>
