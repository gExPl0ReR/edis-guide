EDIS routes all incoming email through a dedicated spam filtering system accessible at [spamfirewall.at](https://www.spamfirewall.at). The filter evaluates each message and assigns it a spam score. Messages that exceed the configured threshold are either flagged or moved to a quarantine folder instead of landing in your inbox. You can adjust the sensitivity thresholds per mailbox to reduce false positives or catch more aggressive spam.

<Steps>

### Log in to the Spamfirewall portal

Open [https://www.spamfirewall.at](https://www.spamfirewall.at) in your browser. Sign in using your **full email address** and the **corresponding mailbox password** — the same credentials you use for IMAP or webmail access.

<Note>
  You need a separate login for each mailbox. The Spamfirewall portal is independent of the vHost management panel and the EDIS customer portal.
</Note>

### Open spam score settings

After logging in, navigate to **Settings** (Einstellungen) → **Spam Score** (Spambewertung). This section lets you define custom spam scoring thresholds for your address.

### Adjust the spam sensitivity

You will see threshold values that determine how aggressively messages are filtered:

- **Tag threshold** — messages scoring above this value have their subject line prefixed with a spam tag (e.g., `[SPAM]`), but are still delivered to your inbox.
- **Quarantine/reject threshold** — messages scoring above this higher value are moved to quarantine and are not delivered to your inbox automatically.

Lower the thresholds to block more spam; raise them if too many legitimate messages are being caught.

<Tip>
  If you are unsure where to start, leave the thresholds at their defaults and check the quarantine folder once a day for a week. This gives you a clear picture of what the filter is catching before you make adjustments.
</Tip>

### Save your custom spam values

Click **Save** or **Apply** to confirm your changes. The new thresholds take effect for incoming messages immediately.

### Check the quarantine folder regularly

The quarantine folder holds messages that were flagged as spam but not permanently deleted. Review it periodically to recover any legitimate emails that were caught by mistake:

1. In the Spamfirewall portal, open the **Quarantine** section.
2. Review the listed messages. Click a subject line to preview the message content.
3. To deliver a message to your inbox, click **Release** or **Deliver**.
4. To permanently delete a quarantined message, select it and click **Delete**.

<Tip>
  Check your quarantine folder at least once a week. Quarantined messages are not held indefinitely — they are automatically purged after a retention period. A legitimate email you are waiting for may already be sitting in quarantine.
</Tip>

### Whitelist trusted senders (if available)

If a specific sender's messages are repeatedly caught by the spam filter, you can add their address or domain to a whitelist. In the **Settings** area, look for **Whitelist** or **Allowed Senders** and add the address there. Whitelisted messages bypass spam scoring and go straight to your inbox.

</Steps>

## Understanding Spam Scores

Every incoming message receives a numerical spam score based on its headers, content, sender reputation, and other heuristics. The higher the score, the more likely the message is spam. EDIS uses SpamAssassin-compatible scoring in its mail system. Typical threshold values are:

| Score range | Default behaviour |
|---|---|
| Below tag threshold | Delivered normally |
| Above tag threshold | Subject tagged, delivered to inbox |
| Above quarantine threshold | Moved to quarantine |

## Contacting Support

If you notice a large number of false positives (legitimate mail in quarantine) or false negatives (spam reaching your inbox) that adjusting the thresholds does not resolve, contact [hosting@edis.at](mailto:hosting@edis.at). Include the sender address, recipient address, timestamp, and the spam score shown in the message headers if possible.
