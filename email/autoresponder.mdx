title: 'Set Up an Autoresponder for Your Email Mailbox'
sidebarTitle: 'Autoresponder'

An autoresponder lets your mailbox reply automatically to every incoming message — perfect for out-of-office notifications, vacation replies, or any situation where you want senders to receive an instant acknowledgement. You configure it entirely inside the EDIS vHost management panel, and it can be enabled or disabled at any time without affecting your mailbox or its stored messages.

<Steps>

### Log in to the vHost management panel

Open [https://manage.edis.at/vhost/](https://manage.edis.at/vhost/) and sign in with your vHost credentials.

### Open the email addresses list

In the left-hand navigation, click **Email Addresses** → **Management**.

### Show all addresses

Under **Display Settings**, choose **Show All** from the dropdown and click **Apply**. The panel now lists every email address configured for your hosting account.

### Find and open your mailbox

Locate the mailbox for which you want to activate the autoresponder. Make sure you select a row with the type **Mailbox (Postfach)** — not a forwarding rule, catchall, or domain row. Click the **edit icon** (pencil) in the **Actions** column.

<Note>
  The red **×** icon next to the edit icon is the delete function. Click the pencil icon, not the red icon, to open the mailbox settings.
</Note>

### Enable the autoresponder

Scroll to the **Autoresponder** section and toggle it to **Active** (aktiviert).

### Enter the autoresponder details

Fill in the available fields:

- **Subject** — the subject line of the automatic reply (e.g., *Out of office: back on 15 January*).
- **Message body** — the text that will be sent to anyone who writes to this address. Keep it concise: state when you will be back, and optionally name a substitute contact. Do not include passwords, internal access links, or sensitive personal data.
- **Date range (optional)** — if the autoresponder supports a start and end date, set these to ensure the reply activates and deactivates automatically.

<Tip>
  A good autoresponder message tells the sender when to expect a reply and who they can contact in an emergency. Avoid repeating your email address or including confidential information.
</Tip>

### Save your changes

Click **Save** to apply the autoresponder. The **AutoResp.** indicator in the address list will show the activation status.

### Verify by testing

Send a test message to the mailbox from a different, external email address. You should receive the automatic reply within a few minutes. If no reply arrives, reopen the mailbox settings and confirm the autoresponder is still marked as active and that the message body is not empty.

</Steps>

## Editing an Existing Autoresponder

To update the reply text or subject line, follow the same steps above: open the mailbox editor, navigate to the **Autoresponder** section, edit the text, and click **Save** again. The status indicator shows whether the autoresponder is active, but it does not display the saved message text — always re-open the editor to review the current content.

## Deactivating the Autoresponder

When you return from your absence, open the same mailbox editor, toggle the autoresponder to **Inactive**, and save. Deactivating it does not delete the saved message body, so you can reactivate it next time without re-entering the text.

<Warning>
  Reply intervals and sender exceptions may apply — for example, each sender may only receive one automatic reply within a defined period. If a sender reports not receiving the reply, confirm the autoresponder is active and test again with a different external address. A single unsuccessful test does not necessarily indicate a broken configuration.
</Warning>

## Troubleshooting

If you cannot locate the autoresponder option, make sure you have opened the correct mailbox row. The top-level domain entry, the customer administration row, and any catchall entries are not individual mailboxes. If the setting is still missing after selecting a mailbox row, contact [hosting@edis.at](mailto:hosting@edis.at) with the affected address and a description of the management level you have opened.
