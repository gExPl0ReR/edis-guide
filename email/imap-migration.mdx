If you are moving to EDIS from another email provider, you do not have to start with an empty inbox. The EDIS IMAP migration tool (powered by Imapsync) copies all your existing messages and folder structure from your old IMAP account directly into your new EDIS mailbox — no technical knowledge required. The tool runs on EDIS infrastructure, so it works entirely server-to-server without needing anything installed on your computer.

## Before you start

Gather the following information for both your old and new mailboxes before beginning the migration:

- **Source (old) account:** IMAP server hostname, port, your email address, and your password.
- **Destination (new) EDIS account:** The EDIS mailbox address you have already created and its password (see [Mailbox Setup](/email/mailbox-setup)).
- Check that the destination mailbox has enough free storage to hold all messages from the source account.

<Note>
  Both IMAP servers must be reachable from the EDIS migration system. If your old mail server is behind a local network firewall or accessible only from specific IP addresses, the EDIS tool may not be able to connect. Contact your previous provider if you are unsure whether their IMAP server is publicly accessible.
</Note>

<Steps>

### Open the EDIS IMAP migration tool

Go to [https://manage.edis.at/imapsync](https://manage.edis.at/imapsync) and sign in if prompted.

### Enter source (old) account details

Fill in the connection details for your existing mailbox:

- **IMAP server:** The incoming mail server of your old provider
- **Port:** Usually `993` with SSL
- **Email address:** Your full address at the old provider
- **Password:** Your password at the old provider

### Enter destination (new) EDIS account details

Fill in the connection details for your new EDIS mailbox:

- **IMAP server:** `mx.edis.at`
- **Port:** `993`
- **Email address:** Your full EDIS email address
- **Password:** Your EDIS mailbox password

### Run a pre-migration sync

Before you switch your domain's MX records over to EDIS, run a first synchronisation. This copies the bulk of your existing messages and folders so the cutover later is quick and minimal.

After the sync completes, check the destination mailbox via [EDIS webmail](https://webmail.edis.at/) to confirm that the expected folders and messages have arrived.

<Tip>
  If you have a large mailbox (several gigabytes), the first sync may take some time. You can start it and come back later to check the result.
</Tip>

### Switch your domain to EDIS (MX records)

Once you are satisfied with the pre-migration sync, update your domain's MX records to point to EDIS so new incoming messages start arriving in your EDIS mailbox. See your DNS documentation for instructions on updating MX records.

### Run a final post-migration sync

After the MX cutover, run the migration tool one more time. This final sync copies any messages that arrived at your old account during the transition window, ensuring nothing is lost.

### Verify and compare

Open both the source account and the destination EDIS mailbox side by side (for example in two browser tabs using webmail for each) and confirm:

- All expected folders are present.
- Message counts and content look correct.
- Sent, Drafts, and Trash folders have been transferred if they were present at the source.

<Warning>
  Do not delete or close your old mailbox until you have completed this verification step. Keep the old account accessible until you are confident the migration is complete and no messages are missing.
</Warning>

</Steps>

## What Imapsync transfers

The EDIS migration tool copies:

- All messages in all IMAP folders
- The folder structure (including nested folders)
- Message flags such as read/unread status

It does **not** transfer:

- Local archives stored only in an email client (these are on your computer, not on the server)
- Contacts or calendar entries (these require a separate migration)
- Email filters or server-side rules configured at your old provider

## Troubleshooting

If the sync fails or you see error messages after the run, check the following:

- **Authentication errors:** Verify the IMAP server hostname, port, and password for the source account. Some providers require an app-specific password if two-factor authentication is enabled.
- **Connection errors:** Confirm that the source provider's IMAP server is publicly accessible on port 993.
- **Storage full:** Check that your EDIS mailbox quota has not been exceeded. Log in to the [vHost panel](https://manage.edis.at/vhost/) to review usage.

For persistent issues, contact EDIS support at [hosting@edis.at](mailto:hosting@edis.at) with the error message, source provider name, and the email address being migrated.
