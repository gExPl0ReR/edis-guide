EDIS email supports CalDAV for calendar synchronisation and CardDAV for contact synchronisation. These open standard protocols let you access and sync your calendars and address books across multiple devices and applications over a secure HTTPS connection. This page provides the server settings and setup instructions for the most common client platforms.

## Server Settings

The CalDAV and CardDAV URLs for your account are personal — they include a path specific to your mailbox, calendar, or address book. Retrieve them from within your webmail interface:

<Steps>

### Log in to EDIS webmail

Open [https://webmail.edis.at](https://webmail.edis.at) and sign in with your full email address and mailbox password.

### Open Mobile Sync settings

Go to **Settings** → **Mobile Sync**. The page displays the exact CalDAV URL for each calendar and the CardDAV URL for each address book in your account.

### Copy your personal URLs

Copy the URL for the resource you want to sync. Do not assume that the CalDAV and CardDAV URLs are the same — they are different paths.

</Steps>

The general connection parameters are the same for all accounts:

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| **CalDAV server URL** | Retrieved from webmail → Settings → Mobile Sync |
| **CardDAV server URL** | Retrieved from webmail → Settings → Mobile Sync |
| **Port** | `443` |
| **Encryption** | TLS / HTTPS (required) |
| **Username** | Your full email address (e.g. `you@yourdomain.at`) |
| **Password** | Your mailbox password |

<Note>
  You must retrieve the server URLs from your own webmail account. The base domain for EDIS CalDAV/CardDAV is `webmail.edis.at`, but the full path includes account-specific identifiers that cannot be guessed from your email address alone.
</Note>

## Client Setup Instructions

### Apple Devices (iPhone, iPad, Mac)

<Steps>

#### Add a CalDAV account (Calendar)

**On iPhone or iPad:**
1. Open **Settings** → **Calendar** → **Accounts** → **Add Account** → **Other** → **Add CalDAV Account**.
2. Enter the following:
   - **Server**: the CalDAV URL from webmail
   - **Username**: your full email address
   - **Password**: your mailbox password
   - **Description**: a friendly name (e.g., *EDIS Calendar*)
3. Tap **Next**. iOS verifies the connection and adds the calendar. Open the **Calendar** app to see your events.

**On macOS (Ventura and later):**
1. Go to **System Settings** → **Internet Accounts** → **Add Account** → **Other** → **CalDAV Account**.
2. Select **Advanced** account type.
3. Enter the server URL, username, and password, then click **Sign In**.

#### Add a CardDAV account (Contacts)

**On iPhone or iPad:**
1. Open **Settings** → **Contacts** → **Accounts** → **Add Account** → **Other** → **Add CardDAV Account**.
2. Enter the CardDAV server URL, your full email address as the username, and your mailbox password.
3. Tap **Next**. Your contacts will begin syncing with the **Contacts** app.

**On macOS:**
1. Go to **System Settings** → **Internet Accounts** → **Add Account** → **Other** → **CardDAV Account**.
2. Enter the server URL, username, and password, then click **Sign In**.

</Steps>

### Android (DAVx⁵)

Android does not have native CalDAV/CardDAV support. Install **DAVx⁵** to enable it.

<Steps>

#### Install DAVx⁵

Download DAVx⁵ from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=at.bitfire.davdroid) or [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/at.bitfire.davdroid/).

#### Create a new account

Open DAVx⁵ and tap the **+** button. Select **Login with URL and username**.

#### Enter your account details

- **Base URL**: paste the CalDAV or CardDAV URL from your webmail settings
- **Username**: your full email address
- **Password**: your mailbox password

Tap **Login**.

#### Select resources to sync

DAVx⁵ discovers all available calendars and address books on the account. Tick the ones you want to sync, then tap **Save account**.

#### Grant permissions

Allow DAVx⁵ to access Contacts and Calendar when prompted. Sync runs automatically in the background.

</Steps>

<Tip>
  DAVx⁵ can discover both CalDAV and CardDAV resources from the same account in a single step. You do not need to create separate accounts for calendars and contacts.
</Tip>

### Thunderbird (Windows, macOS, Linux)

Recent versions of Thunderbird include built-in CalDAV support via the **Calendar** tab (formerly the Lightning extension). CardDAV support for contacts is also integrated.

<Steps>

#### Add a CalDAV calendar

1. In Thunderbird, open the **Calendar** view (the calendar icon in the toolbar or **File** → **New** → **Calendar**).
2. Select **On the Network**, then click **Next**.
3. Select **CalDAV** as the format.
4. Paste your CalDAV URL into the **Location** field.
5. Click **Find Calendars**. Thunderbird connects to the server and lists available calendars.
6. Select the calendars you want to sync and click **Subscribe**.
7. Enter your full email address and mailbox password when prompted for credentials.

#### Add a CardDAV address book

1. Open the **Address Book** (**Tools** → **Address Book** or the people icon).
2. Click **New Address Book** → **Add CardDAV Address Book**.
3. Enter your full email address and click **Continue**.
4. Thunderbird attempts to auto-discover the CardDAV server. If it cannot, enter the CardDAV URL manually.
5. Enter your mailbox password when prompted.
6. Select the address books to sync and click **Subscribe**.

</Steps>

## Windows / Classic Outlook

Classic Outlook for Windows does not include native CalDAV or CardDAV support. Use the **Outlook CalDav Synchronizer** plugin as a third-party solution.

<Steps>

#### Download and install the plugin

Visit [https://caldavsynchronizer.org/](https://caldavsynchronizer.org/) and download the latest release. Install it and restart Outlook.

#### Create a synchronisation profile

In Outlook, click the **CalDav Synchronizer** tab in the ribbon → **Synchronization Profiles** → click **Add** (the **+** icon).

#### Configure the profile

- **Profile name**: a label of your choice
- **Outlook folder**: select the Calendar or Contacts folder you want to sync
- **DAV URL**: paste the CalDAV (for calendar) or CardDAV (for contacts) URL
- **Username**: your full email address
- **Password**: your mailbox password

Click **OK** to save.

#### Run the first synchronisation

Trigger a manual sync from the CalDav Synchronizer ribbon. Check that your calendar events or contacts appear in the Outlook folder.

</Steps>

<Warning>
  The Outlook CalDav Synchronizer plugin supports **classic Outlook for Windows** only. It does not work with the new Outlook experience, Outlook on the web, or Outlook for Mac. Always check [caldavsynchronizer.org](https://caldavsynchronizer.org/) for the current list of supported Outlook versions before installing.
</Warning>

## Important Notes Before Syncing

<Warning>
  CalDAV and CardDAV synchronisation is bidirectional. A deletion made on one synced device propagates to all other connected devices. Back up your local calendars and contacts before connecting a new account for the first time.
</Warning>

After setup, verify the connection by creating a test entry (a new calendar event or a dummy contact) in EDIS webmail and confirming it appears on your device within a few minutes. Then test in the opposite direction. If entries are not syncing:

- Confirm you copied the correct URL (CalDAV ≠ CardDAV URL).
- Check that your username is your **full email address** — not just the local part before the `@`.
- Verify your mailbox password has not changed since setup.
- Check the time zone setting in your calendar application.

For further assistance, contact [hosting@edis.at](mailto:hosting@edis.at) with the device type, application version, protocol used (CalDAV or CardDAV), and any error messages you see.
