Every EDIS mailbox has its own password that is separate from your EDIS customer account password. If you forget your mailbox password, or need to set one for the first time, you can reset it at any time through the vHost panel. You do not need to contact support — the change takes effect immediately.

<Steps>

### Open the vHost panel

Navigate to [https://manage.edis.at/vhost/](https://manage.edis.at/vhost/) in your browser and sign in with your vHost credentials.

<Note>
  Your vHost login is separate from your main EDIS customer account at [manage.edis.at/client/](https://manage.edis.at/client/). Resetting your customer account password does **not** change any mailbox passwords.
</Note>

### Go to Email Address management

In the left-hand menu, click **Email Addresses** → **Management**.

### Show all addresses

Under **Display Settings**, select **Show All** from the dropdown menu, then click **Apply**. This ensures all email addresses associated with your hosting are visible, regardless of domain.

### Find the address and open the editor

Locate the email address whose password you want to change. To the right of the address, click the **edit icon** — it looks like a white page with a yellow pencil.

### Enter the new password

On the edit page, enter your new password in the password field and confirm it. Follow any on-screen guidance about password strength requirements.

<Warning>
  Choose a strong, unique password for your mailbox. A compromised email account can be used to send spam, which may result in your mailbox being temporarily suspended. Never reuse passwords across services.
</Warning>

### Save the change

Click **Save** (or **Change**) to apply the new password.

### Update your email clients

After saving, update the saved password in every email client or application that connects to this mailbox — otherwise those clients will report an authentication error on their next sync.

<Tip>
  To verify the new password is working, log in at [https://webmail.edis.at/](https://webmail.edis.at/) using your full email address and the new password. If webmail works but a desktop client does not, the issue is the saved password in that client, not the mailbox itself.
</Tip>

</Steps>

## Troubleshooting login failures

If you still cannot log in after resetting your password, check the following:

- **Username format:** Your username must be your **full email address** (e.g. `you@example.com`), not just the local part before the `@`.
- **Webmail test:** If webmail at [https://webmail.edis.at/](https://webmail.edis.at/) works but your desktop client does not, the client is using an outdated cached password. Update it in the account settings.
- **Server settings:** Double-check that your client is using the correct [mail server settings](/email/mail-server-settings) — particularly the server hostname and port.
- **Forwarding address:** A pure forwarding address has no mailbox and no password. If the address is a forwarding rule, it cannot be accessed via IMAP or webmail. Check the address type in the vHost panel and change it to a mailbox if needed.

## Security: after suspected unauthorised access

If you believe your mailbox was compromised, a password reset is an important first step — but it may not be sufficient on its own. Also:

- Check your devices for malware.
- Review any forwarding rules set up on the mailbox and remove any you did not create.
- Scan for any unknown mailboxes created under your hosting.
- Change any other accounts that used the same password.
- Report suspicious activity — unknown logins, missing messages, or unrecognised changes — to [hosting@edis.at](mailto:hosting@edis.at).

Do not send your current or new password in a support ticket. EDIS support will never ask for your password.
