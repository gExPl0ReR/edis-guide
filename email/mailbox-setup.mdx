The EDIS vHost panel gives you full control over the email addresses associated with your domain. You can create real mailboxes that store messages on the server, set up forwarding rules that redirect incoming mail to another address, or configure a CatchAll address that catches all unmatched email for your domain. Before you start, make sure your domain is already assigned to a hosting product that includes mailbox capacity — the standalone domain registration ("Aktive Nutzung") does not provide email hosting.

<Steps>

### Check your hosting product

Log in to the [EDIS customer portal](https://manage.edis.at/client/) and review your active services. You need a web hosting plan that includes email accounts. If you only have a domain registration, order a suitable hosting package first.

<Note>
  The number of available mailboxes depends on your hosting plan. Check your plan's specifications before ordering — you may not need a separate package for an additional address if your existing plan has unused capacity.
</Note>

### Open the vHost email management area

Navigate to the [vHost management panel](https://manage.edis.at/vhost/) and sign in with your vHost credentials. In the left-hand menu, click **Email Addresses** → **Management**.

### Filter by domain

Under **Display Settings**, select the domain you want to manage from the dropdown, then click **Apply**. All existing email addresses for that domain are listed.

### Create a mailbox or forwarding rule

Click the **edit icon** (white page with a yellow pencil) next to an existing address, or use the option to add a new address. For each address, choose one of the following purposes:

- **Email Mailbox (Postfach)** — messages are delivered and stored on the EDIS mail server. You can retrieve them via IMAP, POP3, or webmail.
- **Email Forwarding (Weiterleitung)** — incoming messages are redirected to another email address. No messages are stored at EDIS; you cannot log into a forwarding address via IMAP.

Set a strong password if you are creating a mailbox (see [Reset Your Email Password](/email/password-reset) for guidance).

<Warning>
  A pure forwarding address is not a mailbox. You cannot connect an email client to it via IMAP or log into webmail with it. If you need both storage and forwarding, use a mailbox and configure a forwarding rule from within that mailbox.
</Warning>

### Configure a CatchAll address (optional)

A CatchAll catches all messages sent to any address at your domain that is not explicitly defined. To enable it:

1. Go to **Display Settings** and select the domain.
2. Click the **edit icon** next to the domain entry.
3. Enter the destination email address for unmatched messages.
4. Click **Save**.

<Tip>
  CatchAll is useful for catching typos or legacy addresses, but it can attract spam. Consider using it only if you genuinely need to receive mail sent to arbitrary addresses at your domain.
</Tip>

### Wait for provisioning and test

New mailboxes are provisioned within a few minutes. Once ready, test your setup:

- Send a test message **to** your new address from an external account.
- Check that the message arrives in [EDIS webmail](https://webmail.edis.at/) or your email client.
- Send a test message **from** your new address and confirm it is received correctly.

<Note>
  Before deleting any old mailbox, make sure you have backed up any messages you need and confirmed that your new setup is working. Messages stored only locally in an email client are not on the EDIS server and are not migrated automatically.
</Note>

</Steps>

## Planning a mailbox change or migration

If you are changing an existing address rather than creating a new one, clarify a few things before making any changes:

- **Where are existing messages stored?** If they are in a local email client rather than on the server, they will not be moved automatically. Decide how you want to preserve them.
- **Should the old address keep receiving mail?** A deletion does not create a forwarding rule. Set up an explicit forwarding or CatchAll rule if you still need to receive messages at the old address.
- **Avoid redirect loops.** Never forward the new address back to the old one.

## Mailbox storage and quotas

Each hosting plan has defined storage limits, which may apply per mailbox, per account, or as a shared pool. Keep the following in mind:

- Moving messages to the trash does not immediately free up server space — empty the trash folder.
- A local archive in your email client is separate from the server quota.
- An annual hosting plan and a monthly add-on storage subscription have independent billing periods. Cancelling or reducing storage is a separate step from the technical change.

If you are unsure about your current usage, check the webmail interface at [https://webmail.edis.at/](https://webmail.edis.at/), which shows the current mailbox size.
