The **Active Domain Usage** plan is designed for customers who want a domain registration without a full hosting package. It gives you everything you need to point your domain wherever you like — whether that is an external server, a VPS, or a cloud service such as Microsoft 365 — while keeping your DNS fully under your control through the EDIS infrastructure.

## What Is Included

With the Active Domain Usage service, you receive two core capabilities:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="DNS Zone Management" icon="server">
    Full access to the EDIS Anycast DNS panel at [pdns.edis.global](https://pdns.edis.global/login). Create, edit, and delete A, AAAA, MX, TXT, CNAME, and other record types for your domain.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Domain Redirects" icon="arrow-right">
    Set up HTTP redirects directly from the [EDIS customer portal](https://manage.edis.at/client/) — no separate web hosting required. Redirect your domain to any external URL.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Who Should Use This Plan

This plan suits you if any of the following applies:

- You host your website on your own server or VPS and only need DNS management at EDIS.
- Your email is handled by an external provider such as Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace and you need to set the appropriate MX and SPF records.
- You want to redirect a domain to another URL without purchasing a full hosting package.
- You are using EDIS purely as your domain registrar and want to delegate DNS authoratively from the EDIS Anycast network.

## Accessing the DNS Panel

Log in to the DNS panel at [pdns.edis.global](https://pdns.edis.global/login) using your EDIS account credentials. From there you can manage all DNS records for every domain enrolled in the Active Domain Usage service.

## Setting Up Redirects

To configure a redirect from the customer portal:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to the customer portal">
    Navigate to [manage.edis.at/client](https://manage.edis.at/client/) and sign in with your EDIS credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the redirect settings">
    Locate the domain you want to redirect, then open its settings to find the redirect or forwarding option.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the destination URL">
    Provide the full destination URL (including `https://`) and save your changes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the redirect">
    Wait for DNS propagation and then test the redirect in a browser to confirm it resolves correctly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The redirect service through the customer portal may require the **Active Domain Usage** add-on depending on your current domain plan. If the redirect option is not visible in your portal, check whether this add-on is enabled for your domain, or contact support at [hosting@edis.at](mailto:hosting@edis.at).
</Note>
