The EDIS DNS management portal — [PowerDNS-Admin](https://pdns.edis.global/login) — is a standalone platform you use independently from the main customer portal. You log in with your **5-digit EDIS customer number** as the username. Before you can log in for the first time, you need to set a dedicated password for your DNS user through the customer portal.

## Create Your DNS User Password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to the EDIS customer portal">
    Go to [manage.edis.at/client/](https://manage.edis.at/client/) and sign in with your EDIS credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to DNS User settings">
    In the navigation, go to **Domain → DNS User**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a password for PowerDNS-Admin">
    Enter and confirm a new password. This password applies **only** to the PowerDNS-Admin portal — it is separate from your EDIS customer portal password.

    Your password must meet the following requirements:

    - At least **10 characters** long
    - Contains at least one **number**
    - Contains at least one **lowercase letter**
    - Contains at least one **uppercase letter**

    <Note>
      Meeting the minimum requirements alone does not make a password strong. Use a long, unique passphrase that you do not reuse elsewhere, and store it in a password manager.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in to PowerDNS-Admin">
    Go to [pdns.edis.global/login](https://pdns.edis.global/login). Enter your **5-digit customer number** as the username and the password you just set.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## First Login and the OTP Field

When you log in for the first time without two-factor authentication (2FA) configured, leave the OTP field blank. The field appears on the login screen regardless of whether 2FA is active on your account.

If 2FA is already set up for your DNS user, enter the current one-time code from your authenticator app. A lost second factor requires you to go through the supported recovery process — contact [hosting@edis.at](mailto:hosting@edis.at) and do not include OTP codes or recovery keys in your support ticket.

## Enable Two-Factor Authentication

EDIS strongly recommends protecting your DNS portal account with two-factor authentication. DNS records control how your domain resolves across the internet, so securing this access is important.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to PowerDNS-Admin">
    Go to [pdns.edis.global/login](https://pdns.edis.global/login) and sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Click **My Profile** in the top navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to the Authentication tab">
    Select the **Authentication** tab in your profile settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Two-Factor Authentication">
    Click **Enable Two Factor Authentication** and follow the on-screen instructions to scan the QR code with an authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator, Authy, or a compatible TOTP app).

    <Note>
      After clicking "Enable Two Factor Authentication" you may be redirected back to Personal Info. If that happens, click the **Authentication** tab again — the QR code setup screen will now be visible.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your recovery information">
    Store your recovery codes in a safe place. If you lose access to your authenticator app and have no recovery codes, you will need to contact EDIS support to restore access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting Access Issues

**HTTP 403 error or missing domain in the portal**

If the customer portal works but PowerDNS-Admin returns a 403 error, the domain may not be correctly associated with your customer account for DNS management. Waiting for DNS propagation will not fix a missing portal permission. Contact [hosting@edis.at](mailto:hosting@edis.at) with your domain name, customer number, and the exact error message.

**Lost second factor**

If you can no longer use your authenticator app, contact EDIS support to initiate the official recovery process. Do not share OTP codes, setup keys, or passwords in your support ticket. After recovery, re-enable 2FA and verify that you can log in successfully with the new factor.
