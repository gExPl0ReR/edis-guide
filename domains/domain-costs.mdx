EDIS offers straightforward, transparent pricing for domain registrations with no hidden setup fees. The registration price and the annual renewal price are the same, so you always know exactly what you will pay going forward. Alongside the domain itself, all EDIS domains come with access to the EDIS Anycast DNS infrastructure.

## Current Pricing for .at Domains

As of September 2026, the price for registering and annually renewing a `.at` domain at EDIS is:

| Action | Price (incl. 20% VAT) |
|---|---|
| Registration | **EUR 15.00** |
| Annual renewal | **EUR 15.00** |

The listed prices include 20 % Austrian value-added tax (VAT).

<Note>
  Prices are subject to change. The figures above reflect the pricing published on the EDIS domain page in September 2026. Always check the current offer at [edis.at/de/domains](https://www.edis.at/de/domains) before placing an order. The binding price is the one shown at checkout and stated in your contract.
</Note>

## What Is Included

Every `.at` domain registered through EDIS includes the following:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Anycast DNS" icon="network-wired">
    Your domain is served from the EDIS global Anycast DNS network using nameservers **ns1.edis.global** and **ns2.edis.global**, giving you fast and resilient DNS resolution worldwide.
  </Card>
  <Card title="DNS Zone Management" icon="sliders">
    Full access to the DNS administration panel at [pdns.edis.global](https://pdns.edis.global/login) to manage A, AAAA, MX, TXT, CNAME, and other record types.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Info>
  Additional services — such as web hosting, email hosting, or the **Active Domain Usage** add-on — are priced separately and depend on the product you choose. Review the full scope of any product before placing your order.
</Info>
