## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to your customer portal">
    Visit [manage.edis.at](https://manage.edis.at/client/) and sign in with your 5-digit customer number and password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access your vHost area">
    In the customer portal, click **vHost** in the left menu and then click the green arrow next to your webhost to enter the vHost management area.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up your website or email">
    From the vHost area you can configure your website's document root, install WordPress via the App Installer, create email mailboxes, and manage FTP users.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure your DNS">
    Manage DNS records for your domains in the [PDNS-Admin portal](https://pdns.edis.global/login) using your EDIS DNS credentials.
  </Step>
</Steps>
